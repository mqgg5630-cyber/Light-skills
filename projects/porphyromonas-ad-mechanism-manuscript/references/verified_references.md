# Curated and verified reference record (revision v3)

## Scope and verification standard

This is the canonical human-readable record for the expanded 53-reference corpus. The corpus was curated record by record from the prior active manuscript and the user-designated external v0.4 project; it was **not** imported wholesale. Duplicate records, an erroneous correction-note DOI, references traceable only to excluded materials, and references not used by the revised argument were omitted.

For each retained record, title, journal, year, and DOI were compared against the DOI-resolving publisher metadata or DOI landing record available during curation. Cross-repository acceptance does not imply that every substantive result in a cited study was independently reanalysed. Final accountable-author checks should include DOI resolution, Crossmark/correction/retraction status, target-journal style, and verification of any updated 2026 pagination.

## Permitted-use boundaries

- **smORF and proteogenomics [1–5]:** supports discovery/annotation standards and the distinction between prediction, translation, and function; does not validate the current candidates.
- **Oral omics resources [6–12]:** supports oral community, database, and metaproteomic context; does not establish current-cohort expression, disease enrichment, taxonomy, or peptide-spectrum matches without row-level linkage.
- **Predictors and peptide discovery [13–19]:** supports tool identity and validation concepts; model outputs remain operational labels and cannot be converted to measured activity.
- **AD, AChE/PAS, metal, and short peptides [20–34]:** supports mechanistic motivation and experimental precedents; does not prove that the twelve current sequences bind AChE, chelate metals, enter brain, or cause toxicity.
- **Periodontitis–AD epidemiology and causal counterevidence [35–43]:** supports a hypothesis-generating association and causal restraint; does not establish a causal periodontal–AD pathway.
- **Specific bacterial virulence context [44–50]:** supports the plausibility of organism-/factor-specific oral–neural mechanisms; does not assign the current community sequences to any organism.
- **Docking methods [51–53]:** supports interpretation and reproducibility requirements; does not reproduce the external docking or validate its poses/scores.
